# Welcome to TraceDraw Help

TraceDraw is a free, open-source vector drawing program. You use it to draw
illustrations, logos, diagrams, layouts and print work made of shapes, lines
and text that stay sharp at any size, and to combine them with bitmaps. The
same program runs on Windows, macOS and Linux and in a web browser.

This Help explains how to do things in TraceDraw, step by step, and what
every dialog box, panel and tool does.

## How this Help is organized {#organization}

The contents on the left follow the way you work:

- **Get started** introduces the workspace, new drawings, opening and saving.
- **Lines, shapes and outlines**, **Objects, symbols and layers** and
  **Color, fills and transparencies** cover drawing and arranging objects.
- **Special effects**, **Text**, **Templates and styles**, **Pages and
  layout** and **Bitmaps** cover the rest of the drawing work.
- **Printing**, **Web graphics** and **File formats** cover getting your
  work out of TraceDraw.
- **Customize and automate** covers the options, workspaces, scripts and AI
  features, and **Reference** lists every menu command, tool, panel and
  keyboard shortcut.

Use the **Search** tab to find a page by any word it contains, and the
language list at the top to read the same page in another language.

## Getting help inside TraceDraw {#in-the-app}

- Press <kbd>F1</kbd> to open the page about what is in front of you: the
  dialog box that is open, otherwise the panel on show, otherwise the active
  tool.
- Click the **?** button at the bottom of a dialog box or an options page to
  open its page.
- Choose [[menu.help > menu.help.help_topics]] to open this page.
- The [[docker.hints]] panel describes the active tool while you work; its
  [[hint.learn_more]] link opens the tool's page here. See
  [Hints](get-started/hints.md).

Pages open in your web browser, in the language TraceDraw is set to.
