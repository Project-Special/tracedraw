# Glossary

**Active layer**: the layer where new objects go; see
[Objects and layers](../objects/objects-and-layers.md#layers).

**Anchor**: a point of an object that connector lines attach to.

**Artistic text**: text placed at a point, one or a few lines.

**Bleed**: the part of a design that runs past the edge of the page, so that
nothing white shows once the print is trimmed.

**Bitmap**: a picture made of pixels, such as a photo.

**Clip frame**: a closed object that shows only the part of its contents that
lies inside it.

**CMYK**: cyan, magenta, yellow and black, the inks of color printing.

**Curve**: a line or shape made of segments joined at nodes, which you can edit
with the [[tool.shape]] tool.

**Document palette**: the colors used in a drawing, collected under the
drawing window.

**Fill**: what paints the inside of a closed object.

**Flyout**: a group of related tools behind one toolbox button.

**Group**: several objects that move and resize together.

**Guideline**: a line across the drawing window that objects snap to; it does
not print.

**Live effect**: an effect, such as a blend or an envelope, drawn from the
object and changeable at any time.

**Master layer**: a layer whose objects show on every page.

**Node**: a point of a curve where two segments meet.

**Outline**: the line along the edge of an object.

**Panel**: a window of settings docked at the side, such as
[[docker.properties]].

**Paragraph text**: text that flows inside a frame.

**Property bar**: the bar that shows the settings of the selection and the
active tool.

**Resolution**: how many pixels or dots make an inch (dpi).

**RGB**: red, green and blue, the colors of light on screens.

**Segment**: the part of a curve between two nodes.

**Spot color**: a named ink printed on a plate of its own.

**Subpath**: one connected part of a curve made of several parts.

**Symbol**: an object defined once and placed many times as instances.

**Vector**: drawing made of shapes described by points and curves, which stays
sharp at any size.
