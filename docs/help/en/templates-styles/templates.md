# Templates

A template is a drawing to start from: a blank page of a common size, or a
designed business card, flyer, poster, letterhead or label to fill in.
Opening a template makes a new, untitled drawing; the template itself never
changes.

## Starting from a template {#new-from-template}

1. Choose [[menu.file > menu.file.new_from_template]], or click New from
   Template on the Welcome Screen.
2. Find a template:
   - type in [[tpl.search]] to match names and categories;
   - choose where templates come from: [[tpl.all_templates]],
     [[tpl.built_in]] or [[tpl.my_templates]];
   - choose a category, or [[tpl.all_categories]].
3. Click a template to see it. With [[tpl.show_details]] on, the pane on the
   right shows it large with its [[tpl.category]], [[tpl.page_size]] and
   [[tpl.source]].
4. Click [[tpl.open]], double-click the template, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

The [[tpl.thumbnail_size]] slider at the bottom makes the thumbnails larger or
smaller, and [[welcome.open_template]] opens any `.tdt` or `.tdraw` file as a
template.

## The built-in templates {#built-in}

- **Blank pages**: A4 portrait and landscape, A3, Letter, business card
  (90 by 50 mm), A2 poster, web (1920 by 1080 pixels) and social media (1080
  by 1080 pixels).
- **Designed**: two business cards, a sale flyer and an event flyer, a
  poster, a letterhead, a certificate, an invoice, a birthday banner, a quote
  post and a round label. They are made of ordinary shapes and text with
  sample text, so you can change every part.

## Saving your own templates {#save}

1. Make the drawing you want to start from in the future.
2. Choose [[menu.file > menu.file.save_as_template]].
3. Give it a name and save it.

Templates you save appear under [[tpl.my_templates]]. They are kept in the
templates folder of your settings, so they are not available in the browser
version.
