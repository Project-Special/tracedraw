# Objects, symbols and layers

Every shape, line, text and picture in a drawing is an object. These pages
explain how to select objects, move, resize, turn and line them up, how to
order them, group them and organize them in layers, and how to reuse them as
symbols.

They also cover objects with a job of their own: tables, barcodes and QR
codes, comments, and the data you attach to objects.
