# Workspaces

A workspace is how the window is set up: which bars show and where, the
toolbox, keyboard shortcuts, the color palette options and the look of the
interface. Choose one in [[menu.window > menu.window.workspace]], or on the
[[options.workspace]] page of
[[menu.tools > menu.tools.options > menu.tools.options_workspaces]].

![The Workspaces options](../../img/customize-workspaces.png)

## Built-in workspaces {#built-in}

- [[menu.window.workspace_default]]: the usual setup.
- [[menu.window.workspace_lite]]: fewer bars, for a first start.
- [[menu.window.workspace_classic]], [[menu.window.workspace_illustration]] and
  [[menu.window.workspace_page_layout]]: bars and panels for those kinds of
  work.
- [[menu.window.workspace_touch]]: for touch screens. The interface is drawn
  larger, rulers, panels and the status bar are hidden, and two fingers pan
  and pinch to zoom with any tool. Drawings keep their real size.

## What is kept {#kept}

Everything you change is kept when TraceDraw closes and comes back the next
time: the options, where each bar sits and which bars show, the tool each
toolbox group shows, the panels that are open, units, snapping, the settings
new objects take and what the window shows.

## Exporting and importing your setup {#export-import}

To carry your setup to another computer, or keep it safe:

1. On the [[options.workspace]] page, under [[options.workspace_transfer]],
   tick the parts to include: keyboard shortcuts; toolbars, toolbox and status
   bar; color palette options; appearance.
2. Click [[options.workspace_export]] and save the `.tdws` file.
3. On the other computer, click [[options.workspace_import]] and choose the
   file. Only the parts you ticked are applied.

[[menu.window > menu.window.workspace > menu.window.workspace_export]] and
[[menu.window.workspace_import]] do the same with every part.
