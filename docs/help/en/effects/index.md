# Special effects

Effects change how an object looks without changing the object itself: a
blend fills the space between two objects, a contour draws rings around one,
an envelope bends it, an extrusion makes it look solid, a shadow sets it off
the page. Most effects are **live**: the object keeps its own shape, and the
effect is drawn from it, so you can change or remove the effect at any time.

The effects are in the [[menu.effects]] menu, in the Effects flyout of the
toolbox and in their own panels. [Bitmap effects](bitmap-effects.md) change
the pixels of pictures, or of objects drawn as pictures.
