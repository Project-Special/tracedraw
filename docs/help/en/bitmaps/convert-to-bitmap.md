# Converting objects to bitmaps

[[menu.bitmaps > menu.bitmaps.convert_to_bitmap]] (also
[[menu.object > menu.object.convert_to_bitmap]]) turns the selected objects
into one bitmap. Use it to flatten complex artwork, or to prepare objects for
bitmap effects. It is one undo step.

![The Convert to Bitmap dialog box](../../img/bitmaps-convert-to-bitmap.png)

## Settings {#settings}

- **Resolution**: choose from the list (72 to 600 dpi) or type a value (36 to
  2400; 300 at first).
- **Color mode**: Black and White (1-bit), Grayscale (8-bit), Paletted
  (8-bit), RGB Color (24-bit, at first) or CMYK Color (32-bit).
- **Dithered**: for Black and White and Paletted, mixes dots to suggest the
  shades that are missing.
- **Always overprint black**: the black of the bitmap prints over the colors
  beneath.
- **Anti-aliasing** (on at first): smooth edges; off gives hard pixel edges.
- **Transparent background** (on at first); off puts the objects on white.

The dialog box shows how large the bitmap will be, uncompressed.
